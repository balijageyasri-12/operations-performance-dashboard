OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD FRAMEWORK

1. EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD DESIGN
This document outlines the data structure and KPI framework designed for tracking operational efficiency. The backend metrics are powered by the SQL data models included in this repository.

2. CORE KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)
- Turnaround Time (SLA Tracking): Measures the total hours taken from customer ticket opening to resolution. Helps flag workflow delays.
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT): Tracks post-interaction ratings on a scale of 1-5 to evaluate service quality.
- First Contact Resolution (FCR) Rate: Calculates the percentage of incoming issues successfully resolved on the very first interaction without customer follow-up.

3. BUSINESS LOGIC & DATA MAPPING
- Data Source: Raw customer interaction logs, processing time stamps, and agent assignment matrices.
- Target Audience: Operations Managers and Quality Assurance teams.
